1. Resuscitation of a Baby

Scenario: At this station, there is a New Born Baby with Asphyxia.
Instructions:
I. Demonstrate the Resuscitation of a New Born Baby with Asphyxia
II. Move to the next station when the bell rings
Key Areas to AssessScoreDoneNot Done
Washes hands½
Prepares equipment:
• Suction equipment½
• Bag and musk and oxygen source with flow meter and tubing1
• Intubation equipment: Laryngoscope with straight blade½
• Medications½
• Others: Radiant Warmer, Stethoscope, Syringes, Thermometer, IV Cannula, Gloves, Airway Tube, Umbilical Catheters1
Receives the Baby in preformed Towels½
Dry the whole of the baby quickly and remove the wet linen½
Position the baby in neutral position½
Do suction starting with the Mouth, Nose to avoid aspiration1
Insert an ET tube top to ensure open Air way where Necessary1
Provide tactile stimulation flicking the sole of the feet of the baby or rubbing the baby’s back1
Using free flow oxygen by blowing over the infants Nose so that the baby breathes oxygen enriched Air1
Stimulate and maintain the blood circulation by chest compression and or medication
Washes hands½
Total Score: 10
