Course Syllabus

Gynaecology (I)

  • Menstrual disorder (25 min — Free Preview)
  • Ectopic pregnancy (25 min — Free Preview)
  • Cervical erosions, trauma and polyps (15 min)
  • Pelvic inflammatory diseases (16 min)
  • Infertility (25 min)
  • Fistulas (vaginal and rectal) (25 min)
  • Cancers of the female reproductive system (Breasts, uterus, ovary, fallopian tubes). (25 min)

Reproductive Health( I)

  • Safe motherhood (11 min)
  • Family planning (19 min)
  • Sexually transmitted infections including HIV/AIDS (9 min)
  • Adolescent and reproductive health (17 min)
  • Post-abortal care (14 min)
