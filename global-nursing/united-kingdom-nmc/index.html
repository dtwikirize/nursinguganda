Becoming a Registered Nurse in United Kingdom

Fastest international route for Ugandan & African Registered Nurses (Adult, Mental Health, Children's, Midwifery) joining the UK NHS via NMC CBT and practical OSCE.

Open Interactive United Kingdom Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: NMC Test of Competence (Part 1 Computer-Based Test + Part 2 OSCE)

Language Test: IELTS Academic 7.0 (6.5 Writing ok) OR OET Grade B (C+ Writing ok) OR SIFE/Employer route

Lab Unit System: SI Units (mmol/L, g/L, °C) · Timeline: 4–9 Months (Language Test -> CBT -> UK OSCE -> PIN)

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Hold active home registration (e.g., UNMC Registered Nurse / Midwife + Certificate/Diploma/BScN qualification).
  2. Pass English requirement (OET B/C+ or IELTS Academic 7.0 overall — clubbing two sittings within 12 months permitted).
  3. Complete NMC Online Eligibility Application and verify UNMC registration & employer/health character declarations.
  4. Book and pass NMC Part 1 CBT (Numeracy drug calculations 15 Qs + Clinical 100 Qs) at an approved Pearson VUE centre (Kampala/Nairobi).
  5. Obtain UK Health & Care Worker Visa sponsorship, pass the Part 2 Practical OSCE (APIE care stations + clinical skills), and receive your NMC PIN.

Document Checklist

  • OET or IELTS Certificate Meeting NMC Thresholds
  • Active UNMC Registration & Good Standing sent directly to NMC
  • Academic Nursing Transcripts & Employer Reference (3 years check)
  • Police Clearance & Tuberculosis Screening Certificate (UKVI)
