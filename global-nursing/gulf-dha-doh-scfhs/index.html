Becoming a Registered Nurse in UAE & Saudi Arabia (Gulf)

Fast tax-free Middle East pathway for Registered Nurses with >= 2 years hospital experience via DataFlow Primary Source Verification and Prometric exam.

Open Interactive UAE & Saudi Arabia (Gulf) Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: Prometric CBT Nursing Licensing Exam (DHA / MOH / SCFHS)

Language Test: English nursing curriculum & DataFlow Primary Source Verification (PSV)

Lab Unit System: SI / Mixed Units (mmol/L & mg/dL) · Timeline: 2–5 Months (DataFlow PSV -> Prometric Exam -> Eligibility Letter)

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Confirm eligibility under PQR rules (typically Recognized Nursing Diploma/BScN + valid Home License + minimum 2 years continuous post-registration bedside experience).
  2. Submit documents to DataFlow Primary Source Verification (PSV) — school degree, UNMC license, and hospital experience letters.
  3. Book and pass the Prometric CBT exam (~100–150 NCLEX-style MCQs on Med-Surg, Maternal, Paediatrics, Critical Care & Ethics).
  4. Obtain Eligibility Letter and convert to active professional license upon signed hospital offer.

Document Checklist

  • Minimum 2 Years Post-Registration Clinical Experience Letters
  • Active Home License (e.g. UNMC) + Certificate of Good Standing
  • DataFlow PSV Clear Report
  • Prometric Exam Pass Report & Valid Passport
