Becoming a Registered Nurse in Kenya

Registration steps for Kenya-trained KRCHN/BSN nurses and foreign-trained East African nurses seeking Nursing Council of Kenya licensure.

Open Interactive Kenya Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: NCK National Licensure Examination (KRCHN / BSN)

Language Test: English academic instruction verification

Lab Unit System: SI Units (mmol/L, °C) · Timeline: 3–6 Months Verification for Foreign/EAC-Trained Nurses

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Verify secondary school KCSE C (Plain) or international equivalence through Kenya National Qualifications Authority (KNQA).
  2. Create an NCK Online Portal profile and submit foreign training syllabus transcripts plus home country Good Standing verification.
  3. Undergo NCK Credentials & Clinical Placement evaluation if trained outside Kenya.
  4. Pass the Computer-Based NCK Licensure Examination covering Medical-Surgical, Midwifery, Community Health, Psychiatry & Paediatrics.
  5. Apply for NCK Retention / Annual Practice License.

Document Checklist

  • Nursing Diploma/Degree Transcript + Hourly Curriculum Breakdown
  • Home Council License (e.g., UNMC) & Certificate of Good Standing
  • KNQA Foreign Qualification Equated Letter
  • Valid Passport, Clearance Certificate & Passport Photos
