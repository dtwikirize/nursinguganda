Becoming a Registered Nurse in Tanzania

Overview of Certificate, Diploma & BScN nursing registration with Tanzania Nursing and Midwifery Council including East African Community mutual recognition checks.

Open Interactive Tanzania Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: TNMC National Licensing Examination

Language Test: English / Kiswahili clinical proficiency

Lab Unit System: SI Units (mmol/L, °C) · Timeline: 3–6 Months Credentials Check & Licensing Sitting

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Authenticate nursing qualifications through TCU/NACTVET foreign award recognition.
  2. Register on the TNMC Online Information System (TNMC-OIS) and upload training transcripts + Good Standing letter.
  3. Complete any supervised adaptation clinical hours prescribed by TNMC.
  4. Sit and pass the semi-annual TNMC National Nursing & Midwifery Licensing Examination.
  5. Collect TNMC Practicing Certificate prior to hospital deployment.

Document Checklist

  • NACTVET / TCU Foreign Award Equivalence Certificate
  • Official Nursing Transcripts & Clinical Log Summary
  • Current Home Regulatory License & Good Standing Verification
  • Work/Residence Permit (for non-citizens)
