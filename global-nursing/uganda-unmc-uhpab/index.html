Becoming a Registered Nurse in Uganda

Complete guide to becoming an Enrolled Nurse/Midwife or Registered Nurse/Midwife in Uganda: UHPAB assessments, UNMC internship/registration, and CPD renewal.

Open Interactive Uganda Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: UHPAB / UNMEB Certificate or Diploma Theory + Practical & OSCE

Language Test: English instruction verified via accredited Ugandan training institution

Lab Unit System: SI Units (mmol/L, °C, UCG 2023) · Timeline: 2.5 – 4 Years Training + Indexing + UNMC Licensure

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Complete O-Level (UCE) or A-Level (UACE) entry requirements in Biology, Chemistry, Physics/Math, and English.
  2. Enroll in an accredited nursing or midwifery school indexed with UHPAB (formerly UNMEB) and UNMC.
  3. Pass semester theory papers (Section A MCQs, Section B Short Essays, Section C Long Scenarios) plus ward practicals & OSCE.
  4. Apply via the UNMC Digital Portal for Enrolment/Registration Certificate and Annual Practicing License (APL).
  5. Maintain active CPD points and follow Uganda Clinical Guidelines 2023 in clinical practice.

Document Checklist

  • UACE / UCE Academic Transcripts & School Indexing Card
  • UHPAB / UNMEB Final Pass Slip & Academic Transcript
  • Logbook Completion & Clinical Preceptor Sign-off
  • National ID / Passport & Current UNMC APL Receipt
