Becoming a Registered Nurse in United States

Complete US pathway for foreign-educated nurses: CGFNS Credentials Evaluation Service (CES), State Board ATT authorization, NextGen NCLEX-RN, and VisaScreen.

Open Interactive United States Guide

Key Requirements

Qualifying Exam: Next Generation NCLEX-RN (NGN Case Studies & CAT Exam)

Language Test: IELTS Academic 6.5 (Speaking 7.0) or TOEFL iBT or PTE (waivers vary by training country)

Lab Unit System: US Conventional Units (mg/dL, °F, mEq/L) · Timeline: 9–18 Months (CES Evaluation -> ATT -> NCLEX-RN -> VisaScreen)

Step-by-Step Roadmap

  1. Select a target US State Board of Nursing (e.g., New York NYSED, Texas, Florida, Illinois) and check whether CGFNS Certification/CES is mandated.
  2. Have your nursing college & UNMC submit transcripts and license verification straight to CGFNS CES / ERES.
  3. Receive Authorization to Test (ATT) from the State Board + Pearson VUE and pass the adaptive Next Generation NCLEX-RN (70–145 Qs including unfolding clinical case studies).
  4. Pass VisaScreen (ICHP) English & credential check before EB-3 / employment green-card consular interview.

Document Checklist

  • CGFNS CES Professional Course-by-Course Report
  • Official Nursing School Breakdown (Med-Surg, Paeds, Maternity, Psych Theory & Clinical Hours)
  • Home Council License Verification & VisaScreen Certificate
  • English Proficiency Score (IELTS / OET / TOEFL as required by state/VisaScreen)
